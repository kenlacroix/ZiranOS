The idea (concept, not a recipe)

The concept is the same length-confusion bug you already broke in Tier 1: the reader trusts a length your header supplies instead of the real buffer, and leaves file extents unconfined — so an extent that runs past your upload reads server bytes you were never given. Validating against a length the caller supplies is not validation.

How this differs from Tier 1

The secret isn't in your image — the host plants it past your upload, so no local strings/DevTools can reach it. And its offset is per session: on a successful load the kernel prints the exact offset where the secret sits for your instance. Read that line, then craft an extent that reaches it. A fixed offset copied from a writeup won't land — that's the point.
